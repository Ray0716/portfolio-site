Kinematic Synthesis of Planar Leg Mechanisms

← Back to Research

Stony Brook University, Design Intelligence Lab, Stony Brook, NY
Researcher / Simons Research Fellow
May 2025 - Feb 2026

• Developed a computational pipeline for large-scale kinematic synthesis of planar leg mechanisms, generating and evaluating 100 million candidate mechanisms with physics-based simulation

• Implemented geometric filtering and optimization to produce a dataset of 23,000+ novel walking mechanisms; optimized parallel filtering code on the SeaWulf high-performance computing cluster

• First-author paper in MDPI Machines (Q2).
