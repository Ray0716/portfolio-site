Deep Reinforcement Learning for Traffic Light Control

← Back to Research

Frankfurt Institute for Advanced Studies (FIAS), Frankfurt, Germany
Student Researcher
Aug 2024

• Extended an existing deep reinforcement learning framework for coordinated traffic light control, investigating how autonomous agents can optimize signal timing to reduce congestion and improve traffic flow.

• Implemented a reinforcement learning pipeline using SUMO traffic simulation, representing traffic conditions through state matrices incorporating vehicle speed, position, acceleration, and waiting time.

• Trained and evaluated neural network agents across different vehicle arrival probabilities and training durations (50,000–1,000,000 timesteps), analyzing performance through cumulative reward, vehicle throughput, average speed, and waiting time.

• Investigated training dynamics through periodic model checkpointing, comparing agent performance across training stages and identifying potential overfitting and architectural improvements.
